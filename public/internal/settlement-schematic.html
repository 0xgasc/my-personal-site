That’s it. Everything below is the math and the options.

How do we spend treasury money privately?

Our shared treasury already hides per-merchant flows from public observers. The question is: when we move money OUT of the treasury, how visible is that? Here are the options, from most visible to most private.

Ranked best → worst Privacy · Cost · Legal
1. Shared treasury commingling (current design)Single-use deposits sweep into one shared wallet. Observer sees total volume but can’t attribute outflows to specific merchants without your database. Privacy by architecture, not by tool. Already built, zero extra cost. Moderate · free · Clean
2. CEX off-rampDeposit USDC to exchange, withdraw to different wallet or convert to fiat. Breaks the on-chain link — withdrawals come from the CEX’s omnibus pool. Exchange has full KYC on you. Chainalysis has 1B+ address clusters. On-chain privacy is real; operational privacy is zero. High on-chain · $1–25 · Clean
3. Privacy Pools (0xbow)Live on Ethereum mainnet since Mar 2025. ZK proofs let you prove funds aren’t from sanctioned sources without revealing which deposit is yours. Vitalik-endorsed, EF-integrated (Kohaku wallet). Compliant by design. ETH/USDC/USDT/DAI supported. High · gas only · Clean
4. Veil Cash (Base L2 shielding)Privacy protocol on Base L2. Deposit USDC → Groth16 ZK-shielded UTXO balance → withdraw to any address via relayer. 0.3% deposit fee, no withdrawal fee. Compliance screening via 0xbow + Coinbase EAS / Binance BABT auto-approval. Native USDC support. Newer and smaller than Railgun but on a cheaper chain with built-in compliance. Very high · 0.3% · Clean
5. Railgun (on-chain shielding)Shield USDC into private balance → unshield to fresh address. Proof of Innocence blocks illicit funds (rejected $9.5M zkLend hack live, Feb 2025). Ethereum, Arbitrum, Polygon, BNB. $5.16B+ cumulative shielded volume. EF-endorsed. Very high · 0.25% · Clean-ish
6. Umbra (stealth addresses)One-time address for the recipient — payment isn’t linkable to their known address. 350k+ txs, ~$500M total. Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Base. Hides receiver only (amounts + sender still visible). Receiver only · gas · Clean
7. Privacy coin conversion (XMR)USDC → XMR via ChangeNOW or MEXC. Ring signatures hide sender, amount, recipient. Round-trip: 0.3–0.5% via CEX, 2–4% via no-KYC swaps. 73 exchange delistings in 2025. Still on MEXC ($155M/day), KuCoin, Kraken US. EU AMLR Art.79 bans CASP handling by Jul 2027. Very high · 0.3–4% · Elevated
8. Tornado CashOFAC sanctions removed Mar 2025 (Fifth Circuit ruling). Contracts functional. But no compliance filtering — no way to prove funds are clean. Developer Storm convicted (MTB), Pertsev serving 5yr. Reputational poison. Very high · 0.1–1% · Toxic

Bottom line: our shared treasury already does most of the work. Competitors can see our total volume but not per-merchant breakdowns. That covers the most realistic threat.

The practical playbook: Shared treasury (free, already built) + CEX off-ramp when we need fiat (KYC’d, clean paper trail). If a merchant asks for extra privacy, offer XMR opt-in. Everything else on this list is either experimental, legally risky, or solving a problem we don’t have yet.
